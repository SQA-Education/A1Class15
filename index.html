📖 1. Reading Comprehension

A Week with Maria

Hello! My name is Maria. I am Colombian. I am 28 years old. I am a nurse. I work at a hospital near my house.

Every day I wake up at 6:00 AM. I eat breakfast and go to work. After work, on Tuesdays and Thursdays, I study English.

On Saturdays, I go shopping. The mall is across from the park. I like to buy blue dresses — these are my favorite clothes! This skirt is new, and those shoes are also new.

Do you go shopping on weekends? Does your family go with you? How much are your favorite clothes?

✍️ 3. Writing

Writing Task — Tell us about yourself and your week

Write 6-8 sentences. Include ALL of these:

  • Your name, age, and where you are from (nationality)
  • Your job or school
  • One thing you do in your daily routine (I wake up, I eat, I go to...)
  • A place near your home (supermarket, park, bank, pharmacy...)
  • One sentence with this, that, these, or those

Example: "My name is Ana. I am Colombian. I am 30. I am a teacher. I wake up at 7 AM. I go to the supermarket near my house. These are my favorite books."
